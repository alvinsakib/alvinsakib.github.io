Hi! I am **MD. Alvin Sarkar Sakib**, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from **Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh**. I am currently working as an **Intern Engineer at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh**, where I explore areas related to **Large Language Models, on-device AI, and agentic AI systems**.

My research interests include **Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic and Multi-Agent AI Systems, AI Evaluation and Benchmarking, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Medical Image Analysis**. My undergraduate research focused on **fetal brain plane classification using attention-enhanced transfer learning**, combining deep learning architectures with attention mechanisms for medical image analysis.

I am particularly interested in developing **reliable, efficient, and collaborative intelligent systems** and exploring how AI models can be effectively evaluated and deployed in real-world environments.

My work and projects involve **machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, LLMs, on-device AI, and full-stack software development**. I am also interested in research opportunities that combine **NLP and computer vision** to solve meaningful real-world problems.

You can find my projects and research activities through my **GitHub** and connect with me through **LinkedIn**.
